# Judge walkthrough

TL;DR: Open [Agent Stage](https://agent-stage-psi.vercel.app/) and inspect Quiet Truth. Playback, source declarations, public Meta feedback, registration evidence and provisional standings are visible without an administrator invitation.

## One complete demonstration

1. On **The stage**, choose **AI**, then open **Quiet Truth (Trap Beat Remix)**. Play the finished track or an individual ingredient. The supplied music is AI-generated according to G2's declaration; do not infer human origin from a valid upload signature.
2. Read **Every layer has a story**. The app distinguishes source declarations, signature validity and signer trust. Its pilot signer is untrusted; the signature records upload and ingredient relationships rather than proving authorship or legal rights.
3. Read **Experimental Meta feedback**. Muse Spark 1.2 compared the full seed and remix analysis audio. Four ratings have written rationales, audio durations and a saved provider response ID. This feedback is non-voting and has not been validated by an independent human reviewer. Opening it does not make another model request.
4. Follow **View IP registration** and **Registration receipt**. The remix is registered on Aeneid, chain 1315, as a derivative of its seed. A separate real test license, token **73230**, was minted: [license mint receipt](https://aeneid.datanetscan.io/tx/0xee47c1f4e6616088f608f3c1f93726d7f993af72ce0c53a4afa1d5084136204f). Its receiver is an app-managed test account. No mainnet purchase or commercial proceeds are claimed.
5. Open **Leaderboard**. Human vote counts, competitive agent scorecards and jury decisions remain separate. There is no fabricated reviewer or winner. First Chapter is the original voting event; Open Session is a separate event for new submissions.

## Signed-in capabilities

Privy email/wallet sign-in creates or resolves a listener account. Existing invited artists and organizers link Privy to their stable account to retain permissions. An artist invitation is required to submit; no administrator code or shared buyer credential is distributed in this repository.

Artists and admins can open saved performance transcripts and copy/download the recognized words. The notification bell provides a persistent inbox. Background push requires each device's explicit permission; its delayed test checks actual device delivery. Do not interpret server configuration as proof that a physical phone received an alert.

The license holder's designed certificate and download remain private to the holder. The video can demonstrate G2's own certificate without exposing login codes. Public chain receipts establish the test transaction without requiring access to a buyer account.

## Boundaries

Competitive judging requires an eligible reviewer owner and assignment. Mainnet payments, paid license checkout, contributor claims, cash rewards, custom agent webhooks, Kleros and Jev are outside the demonstrated pilot. Public sign-in is not Sybil-proof identity. Check the live UI for current dates and participation rather than treating a static screenshot as current results.
